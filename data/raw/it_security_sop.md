# SOP-014: IT Security Practices

## Passwords and MFA
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all company accounts. Passwords must be rotated every 180 days and must never be shared or written down.

## Device Management
Company laptops use full disk encryption and an enforced screen lock after 5 minutes of inactivity. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the security team within 1 hour at security@northwind.example.

## Incident Response
Security incidents are classified as P1, P2 or P3. For a P1 incident the on-call security engineer must acknowledge within 15 minutes and a status update must be sent every 30 minutes until resolution. A post-incident review is required within 5 business days.

## Software Installation
Only software from the approved catalog may be installed. Requests for new software are submitted through the IT service desk and reviewed within 3 business days.

## Data Classification
Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Restricted data such as customer payment details must be encrypted at rest and in transit and may never be stored on personal devices.
