# Northwind Labs - Employee Handbook

## Working Hours
Core working hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time. Employees are expected to be available during core hours and may schedule the remaining hours flexibly. The standard working week is 40 hours.

## Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Fully remote arrangements require approval from the VP of People. Remote employees receive a one-time home office stipend of 500 USD.

## Probation
New employees complete a 90 day probation period. Performance is reviewed at 30, 60 and 90 days. Either party may end employment during probation with 7 days notice.

## Performance Reviews
Formal performance reviews are held twice a year, in June and December. Reviews combine self assessment, manager feedback and peer feedback. Salary adjustments are decided after the December review.

## Learning Budget
Every employee has an annual learning budget of 1,200 USD for courses, books and conferences. Unused budget does not roll over.

## Code of Conduct
Harassment, discrimination and retaliation are not tolerated. Concerns can be reported to the People team or anonymously through the ethics hotline at ethics@northwind.example.
