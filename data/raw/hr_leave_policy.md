# Northwind Labs - Leave Policy

Northwind Labs is a fictional company created for demonstration purposes.

## Annual Leave
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly at 2 days per month. Up to 10 unused days may be carried over to the next year; any days beyond that expire on 31 March.

## Sick Leave
Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days. Sick leave does not carry over.

## Parental Leave
Primary caregivers are entitled to 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers are entitled to 6 weeks of fully paid leave. Parental leave can be taken in one block or split into two blocks within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

## Requesting Leave
Submit annual leave requests in the HR portal at least 14 days in advance. Requests longer than 10 consecutive working days need approval from both the line manager and the department head.

## Public Holidays
The company observes 11 public holidays each year. The full list is published in the HR portal every December.
